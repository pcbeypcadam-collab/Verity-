Privacy Policy for Verity

Last Updated: November 2025

Welcome to Verity. We take your privacy seriously. This policy outlines how our Discord bot ("Verity", "we", "us") collects, uses, and protects your information when you interact with it on Discord servers.

1. Information We Collect

To provide our moderation, chat, and utility features effectively, Verity may process the following types of data:

  • Server and User IDs: Used to save server-specific settings, user preferences, and to execute commands accurately.
  • Message Content: Processed solely in real-time to detect and respond to commands, and to maintain chat interactions. We do not permanently store the content of your everyday conversations.
  • Member Data: Information regarding server joins/leaves and roles, necessary for welcome messages, auto-roles, and server logging.

2. How We Use Your Information

All collected data is strictly used to make Verity function properly. This includes:

  • Enabling moderation tools (e.g., logging, mute/kick commands).
  • Responding to user commands and facilitating chat interactions.
  • Maintaining server configurations so you don't have to set them up repeatedly.

3. Data Sharing and Third Parties

We do not sell, rent, or trade your data to third parties. Your information is only accessed by the bot's system to provide the promised features. We do not share your data with advertisers.

4. Data Retention and Deletion

We only keep data for as long as it is necessary to provide the bot's services. Message content is not stored long-term unless it is required for a specific logging feature enabled by server administrators.

If you wish to have your data deleted from our database, or if a server owner wants to remove all server settings, you can request this by joining our support server or emailing us.

5. Your Rights

You have the right to request information about the data we hold about you, and the right to request its deletion. We comply with data protection regulations by ensuring users have control over their stored data.

6. Contact Us

If you have any questions about this Privacy Policy, or if you need to request data deletion, please contact us at inekturkiye@gmail.com.
