Privacy Policy for Verity Bot

This Privacy Policy explains how Verity ("the Bot") collects, uses, and protects user data.

1. Data We Collect:

User IDs and Server IDs: To store server-specific settings and user preferences.

Message Content: To process and respond to chat commands. Messages are not stored permanently unless required for a specific feature.

Member Data: To manage roles, welcome new members, and perform moderation actions.

2. How We Use Data:

To provide and improve the Bot's features (moderation, chat, utilities).

To ensure the Bot functions correctly within your server.

3. Data Sharing:

We do not sell or share your data with third parties.

4. Data Deletion:

Users can request the deletion of their data by contacting us at [inekturkiye@gmial.com].

5. Contact:

For any questions, please contact us at [inekturkiye@gmial.com] or join our support server.
